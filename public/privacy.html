Privacy notice

Ledger is a training log run by Coach Leonidas. This notice explains what it stores, who can see it and how to remove it.

What Ledger stores

  • Your account: name, email address, a salted hash of your password (or a Google account identifier if you sign in with Google).
  • Your training: programs, sessions, sets, weights, reps, effort, notes, bodyweight and the feedback you give about pump, workload and soreness.
  • If you fill it in: your intake profile (goals, experience, injuries, schedule).
  • Messages between you and your coach.
  • Technical data: the type of device you are signed in on, and error reports that contain no training data.

Bar path videos are analysed on your device. Only the numbers they produce are stored with the set, never the video.

Who can see it

  • You can see everything in your account.
  • If you join a coach with their code, that coach can read your log, progress, intake profile and messages, and can send you programs, feedback and changes to your plan. You can leave your coach at any time under More, Account, and they lose access.
  • The person who runs Ledger can access the database to keep the service working.
  • Nobody else. The data is not sold and not used for advertising.

Services that handle it

  • Cloudflare hosts the app, the database and the email that Ledger sends.
  • Google, only if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • GitHub hosts the Android app downloads. The app asks it only whether a newer version exists.

Your choices

  • Export: More, Your data, Backup (JSON) or Log as CSV.
  • Delete: More, Account, Delete account removes your account, your synced data and your messages from the server. A copy stays on your device until you erase it there.
  • Email: coaching emails (new feedback, new program) can be switched off under More, Account.
  • Devices: More, Account lists where you are signed in and lets you sign out of any of them.

Under data protection law (GDPR) you also have the right to access, correct and erase your data and to complain to your data protection authority. The deletion and export tools above cover access and erasure directly. For anything else, ask your coach, who can pass it on to the operator.

How long it is kept

Until you delete your account. Deleting an account removes it from the live database; backups are kept for up to 30 days and then expire.

Changes

If this notice changes in a way that matters, Ledger asks you to read and accept it again.
