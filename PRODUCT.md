# Product
Ledger is a personal, offline-first training log for one lifter (Leonidas, Coach Leonidas), later opened to coaching clients. Register: product. Mode: Operate.
Users run it one-handed in a gym between sets, then review volume and progress at a desk.
Principles: speed of entry over decoration; the plan is the user's own sheet; numbers are tabular and never truncated; plate colours carry meaning (red near failure, green stays in reserve).
Assumptions: inferred from the brief and the user's workbook, not from an interview.
